🏪 About Us

Serving Bodinayakanur Families Since 2010

Rani Hyper Market started with a simple dream — to bring the freshest, most authentic groceries to every household in Bodinayakanur at the fairest prices.

What began as a small store on Main Road has grown into Bodinayakanur's most trusted supermarket, stocking over 7,000 products including fresh farm produce, authentic Tamil Nadu masalas, premium FMCG brands, daily dairy, and household essentials.

Today, with our digital ordering platform and WhatsApp delivery service, we bring Rani Hyper Market right to your doorstep — blending the warmth of a neighborhood store with the convenience of modern e-commerce.
