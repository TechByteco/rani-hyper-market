● Chapter 05 · Mathematical Rigor

Live Financial Formulas: Margin Protection & Conversion Models

Replacing arbitrary intuition with verifiable mathematical retail equations.

Core Formulas
The Retail Mathematics Engine

Deal Price = MRP × (1 - Discount% / 100)

Unit Profit = Deal Price - Wholesale Cost

Gross Margin% = (Unit Profit / Deal Price) × 100

🛡️ Margin Check: Enforces Gross Margin ≥ 5.0%

Conversion% = min(22.0%, 4.5% + (Discount% × 0.35%))

Projected Orders = Audience Reach × (Conversion% / 100)

Projected GMV = Projected Orders × Deal Price

ROI Multiplier = Projected GMV / Dispatch Cost
